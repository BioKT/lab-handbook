# Journal Club rota

The long slot of the BIOKT group meeting, **Tuesdays 11:45–12:25**. It is a journal
club by default and a project talk when there is a specific reason; which one is
agreed in your individual meeting the week before. If it is a paper, it goes on
Slack **the Friday before**.

The rota cycles through one list:

`David Ruiz → Sourish → Juan Carlos → Lydia → David De Sancho → Xabier`

Skipping a date moves the whole queue on by a week; nobody loses a turn. After a
session, move its row to [`papers.md`](papers.md).
