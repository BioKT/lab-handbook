# BIOKT Journal Club

An essential part of the science we do is the science we read.

The journal club takes the long slot of the weekly BIOKT group meeting, unless
there is a reason for a project talk instead.

| File | Contents |
|------|----------|
| [`rota.md`](rota.md) | Upcoming sessions: who presents when, and the paper once chosen |
| [`papers.md`](papers.md) | Every paper presented since January 2020, newest first |

## Presenting

1. Find your slot in `rota.md`.
2. Once you have chosen the paper, add its full citation and DOI to your row, and
   post it on Slack the Friday before.
3. After the session, move the row to the top of the right year in `papers.md`.

Edits can be made directly on GitHub. Papers are listed by DOI only; do not commit PDFs.
